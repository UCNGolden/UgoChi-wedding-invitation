Order of the Day

Wedding Timeline

09:30 AM

Arrival of Guests

Guests seated in quiet reflection and prayer at Our Lady of Lourdes Parish, Maryland, Enugu.

10:00 AM

Solemnization of Holy Matrimony

Bridal Procession, Exchange of Nuptial Vows, Holy Mass, Ring Blessing, and Nuptial Blessing.

12:30 PM

Traditional Marriage Rites

Cultural procession, formal greetings, Wine-carrying ceremony (Igba Nkwu), and parental blessings.

02:00 PM

Nuptial Banquet & Feast

Sumptuous feast featuring classic delicacies, refreshments, and joyful hospitality at Jideofor's Compound.

03:30 PM

Cutting of Cake & First Dance

Cutting of the royal wedding cake, solemn champagne toast, and the couple's magical first dance.

04:30 PM

Celebration & Dance Floor Opens

High-energy African rhythms, couple money-spray celebration, photographs, and evening merriment.
